Family Law Services for Overseas Indians

At NRI Legal Services, we understand the dilemma of dealing with family issues and concerns, especially when living far from home. Distance & unfamiliarity with the Indian legal system can be extremely challenging for Indians overseas. Our professional teams handle such matters with empathy and help you navigate the complex landscape of family law. We are committed and dedicated to safeguarding your rights and ensuring complete support every step of the way.

Our Family Law Services

The Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007 (MWPSC Act)

Citizens Act, 2007 (MWPSC Act) Normally parents execute transfer deeds in favour of their sons daughters etc. While they are alive with the condition that the beneficiaries shall maintain and take care of them and later resile from their commitments and obligations. Old parents are legally entitled to get the transfer deeds cancelled/nullified and further get maintenance from such sons and daughters. When Transfer deeds are executed one has to ensure that this clause is inserted in the transfer deed that children shall maintain and take care of you. Such a condition in the deed is a vital must to ensure your protection especially in the old age .
